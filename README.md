# Vrinda-Store-Sales-Analysis-Dashboard
This project involves analyzing the annual sales performance of Vrinda Store in the year 2022, using Microsoft Excel. The objective was to transform raw sales data into an interactive dashboard that provides insights into customer demographics, sales trends, order status, geographical performance, and sales channel effectiveness in order to make more data driven decisions for driving sales performance for 2023.

## Tools and Technologies Used
+ Microsoft Excel
+ Data Cleaning
+ Pivot Tables
+ Pivot Charts
+ Slicers
+ Dashboard Design
+ Data Visualization

##  Business Questions Answered

## 🔍 Business Problems Explored

This project wasn't just about crunching numbers; it was about uncovering insights that could drive business growth and improve decision-making. 📈

### 📈 Comparative Sales & Order Analysis
Analyzed the relationship between total sales revenue and order volume throughout the year to identify seasonal trends and performance fluctuations.

### 📅 Identifying Peak Sales Months
Determined which months generated the highest sales and order counts, providing valuable insights for future inventory planning and marketing campaigns.

### 👩‍🦰 Gender-Based Purchasing Analysis
Explored purchasing behavior across genders to understand which customer segment contributed the most to overall sales revenue.

### 📦 Order Status Performance
Evaluated the distribution of delivered, cancelled, returned, and refunded orders to assess operational efficiency and customer satisfaction.

### 🗺️ Top Revenue-Generating States
Identified the highest-performing states contributing to overall revenue, helping businesses focus regional marketing and expansion efforts.

### 👥 Age & Gender Relationship Analysis
Investigated purchasing patterns across different age groups and genders to understand key customer demographics and target audiences.

### 🌐 Sales Channel Contribution Analysis
Compared sales performance across multiple online platforms such as Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart to identify the most effective sales channels.

### 🏆 Category & Customer Insights
Analyzed customer preferences and category performance to understand demand patterns and support product strategy decisions.

## Data Cleaning and Transformation
This stage began with getting to know the dataset and checking for any data quality issues.

- Checking NULL values, blanks and Errors
- Removing duplicates, and irrelevant columns.
- Made sure data is consistent and clean with respect to data type, data format and values used.
- Creating 2 new attributes 'Age Group' and 'Month' were added for logical and easy interpretation of dataset.

## Data Analysis
Six pivot tables were created to summarise the data and help identify trends in the dataset focusing on relationship between sales and other factors such as gender, orders in diff months, top states. Trends in order status, orders on diff channels orders and age comparison were also performed.

## Data Visualization
Finally, the dashboard was created by inserting and customizing the pivot charts of corresponding pivot table. For user friendly and interactive experience 3 "Slicers" were added.

## Key Insights
### 1. 📅Sales Trend Analysis
Sales peaked during the first quarter of the year, with March recording the highest revenue and order volume.
A gradual decline in sales was observed during the later months of the year.
### 2. 🚺Customer Demographics
Women contributed approximately 64% of total sales.
Men accounted for approximately 36% of total sales.
Adult women represented the largest customer segment.
### 3. 📦Order Fulfillment Performance
Around 92% of orders were successfully delivered.
Returned, cancelled, and refunded orders collectively represented a small percentage of total orders.
### 4. 🏞️Geographic Performance
Top performing states by sales:
1. Maharashtra
2. Karnataka
3. Uttar Pradesh
4. Telangana
5. Tamil Nadu
### 5. 👩Age and Gender Analysis
Adult customers generated the highest share of sales.
Women consistently outperformed men across all age categories.
### 6. 🛍️Channel Performance
Top sales channels:

1. Amazon (35%)
2. Myntra (23%)
3. Flipkart (22%)

These three platforms contributed the majority of total orders.

## Recommendations based on the Analysis
To boost sales in the future:

🎯 Targeted Approach: Based on our data analysis, there's a golden opportunity to engage women aged 30-49 in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh. 🚺 With this segment driving a significant portion of sales, tailoring marketing efforts could unlock remarkable growth potential.

📱 Online Strategy: Harnessing the power of online platforms, particularly Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra, will be a game-changer. 🛒💻 Utilizing these channels for targeted ads and promotions can maximize reach and customer engagement.
